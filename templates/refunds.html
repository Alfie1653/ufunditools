Refund Policy

1. Successful purchases

Once your file has been delivered via Telegram, the sale is final. Digital products cannot be "returned" the way physical goods can.

2. If you paid but didn't receive your file

This can happen occasionally due to payment processing delays. Before contacting us, try:

  • Waiting a few minutes and checking Telegram again
  • Using the "Lost Your Link?" button on the notes page with the phone number you paid with

If neither works, contact us through the Support button and include your phone number and roughly when you paid. We'll look into it and either deliver your file or refund you.

3. If your M-Pesa was charged but the order shows as failed or pending

Occasionally M-Pesa and our payment provider can be out of sync. If this happens to you, contact Support with your M-Pesa confirmation message -- we'll verify with our payment provider and make it right.

4. How refunds are issued

Refunds, when applicable, are sent back to the M-Pesa number used for the original payment.

5. Contact

All refund and delivery issues are handled through the Support button on the site.

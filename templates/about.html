About Ufundi Tools

Josam, Creator

Ufundi Tools is a small, student-run platform curating engineering notes, past papers, and study material for university students.

As a civil engineering graduate, Ufundi Tools started from something that actually happened on campus.

In the course of my studies, I took a Computer Programming course where we were taught C, but the exam tested us on C++. I remember being genuinely angry -- not at anyone in particular, just at the gap between what we were taught and what we were expected to know. So I went out and taught myself.

w3Schools first, then YouTube, hours of it, filling in what the classroom notes never covered. That's actually where my programming journey properly started despite never learning it in class. I am not yet a proficient programmer, but I am committed to continuous learning and improvement.

That experience repeated itself more than once. Class notes were a guide, not the full picture. After lectures, I'd go find the rest -- watch videos, read further, cross-reference until a topic actually made sense.

Ufundi Tools exists because I don't think every student should have to go through that scramble alone, especially not the night before an exam. The notes here are curated the way I wish notes had been curated for me -- researched properly, explained clearly.

Every unit on this site is researched, written, and organized by hand -- not scraped or resold. If you don't see a topic you need, you can request it, and we'll work on curating it for you.

Payments are handled securely via M-Pesa, and your notes are delivered straight to you on Telegram within moments of paying.

How It Works

Buying notes takes less than a minute. Watch a quick walkthrough below, or follow the steps.

Buying & Getting Your Notes

Downloading via Browser

1

Find the notes you need

Go to Browse Notes and search or scroll to find your unit. Use the sidebar to search by name or sort by price. If you don't see what you need, you can request it -- more on that below.

2

Click BUY

On the unit's card, click the BUY button. A small window will pop up asking for your M-Pesa phone number.

3

Enter your phone number and pay

Type in your Safaricom number (e.g. 07XXXXXXXX) and click Pay. Within a few seconds, you'll get an M-Pesa prompt on your phone. Enter your M-Pesa PIN to complete the payment.

4

Wait for confirmation

The site will show "Waiting for payment confirmation..." while it checks with M-Pesa. This usually takes just a few seconds.

5

Get your file

Once payment is confirmed, two buttons will appear: Open Telegram and Download PDF. Use either one -- Telegram sends the file to our bot chat, while Download PDF saves it straight to your device.

Important: both buttons disappear after about 15 seconds, and the Download PDF button only works once. If you miss it, don't worry -- your file is still safe. Use Lost Your Link? to get your Telegram link back at any time.

A few extra things worth knowing

Didn't get your file?

Use the Lost Your Link? button on the notes page and enter the phone number you paid with -- this recovers your order instantly in most cases.

Don't see your unit listed?

Click Request a Topic and tell us what you need. We'll research and upload it.

Something went wrong?

Click the Support button in the bottom corner of any page and send us a message with your phone number -- we'll get back to you.
